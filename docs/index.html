The clipboard should remember. You should not have to.

Clipth records text, images, video, files, links, and rich text. Re-copying an existing item moves it forward instead of making another copy.

Apple NLEmbedding and Vision keep semantic search and OCR on-device. Sensitive values are conservatively redacted across the interface, exports, and MCP by default.

Feature atlas

Much more than a clipboard list.

From the moment something is copied to the moment it is found, transformed, pasted, protected, or synced, Clipth keeps the whole workflow native and under your control.

01

Capture

Whatever you copy, it belongs in the same memory.

Text Images Video Files Links Rich text
  • Re-copy an existing item and it moves forward—no duplicate clutter.
  • Compressed image storage and external payloads keep large histories light.
  • Hover previews, video thumbnails, and inline playback reveal content before you reuse it.

The cloud is a choice, not a prerequisite.

Sync is off until you configure it. When enabled, Clipth encrypts incremental operations on-device with AES-256-GCM and merges concurrent edits instead of replacing one Mac’s database with another.

Read the sync architecture →

  2. 02Store where you choose

    iCloud Drive, WebDAV, S3-compatible storage, or a local folder.

  3. 03Merge, do not overwrite

    Field versions, encrypted journals, and recoverable conflict copies protect concurrent work.
