## en

### Renamed to Clipth

- The app's English name is now **Clipth** (the Chinese name 剪迹 is unchanged), with a refreshed app icon. This is a rename only — your clipboard history and settings are preserved and recovered automatically on first launch.
- Because macOS ties the Accessibility (auto-paste) permission to the app's identity, you may need to grant it once more after this update: open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, remove any leftover rows, and enable the currently running Clipth. Auto-paste keeps working across future updates afterward.

### New

- **Custom accent color** — pick any color as the app accent alongside the built-in warm presets. Your choice is saved and applied to content tints across the app, while the paper surfaces stay the same.
- **Replay the walkthrough** — re-open the guided main-window tour anytime from Settings to revisit search, groups, the monitoring toggle, and Quick Paste.

## zh

### 更名为 Clipth

- 应用英文名现为 **Clipth**（中文名「剪迹」保持不变），并更新了应用图标。本次仅为更名——你的剪贴板历史与设置会自动保留，并在首次启动时恢复。
- 由于 macOS 会把「辅助功能」（自动粘贴）授权绑定到应用标识，本次更新后你可能需要重新授权一次：打开 系统设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 辅助功能，移除残留的旧条目，并勾选当前运行的 Clipth。此后的更新都会稳定保留授权。

### 新功能

- **自定义强调色** —— 除内置的暖色预设外，现在可以选择任意颜色作为应用强调色。所选颜色会被保存并应用到全局的内容着色，而纸张质感的界面保持不变。
- **重播使用引导** —— 随时可在设置中重新打开主界面导览，回顾搜索、分组、监听开关与快速粘贴。
