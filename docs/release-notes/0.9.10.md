## en

### Reliability

- Expanded clipboard-history recovery to scan likely Application Support and ClipTrace-related container locations for legacy SwiftData stores.
- Recovery still only imports files that contain ClipTrace's `ZCLIPBOARDITEM` table, and rows are deduplicated by clipboard-item UUID.
- This covers machines where the pre-macOS-26 history store was left in a non-standard or older app-container path.

## zh

### 可靠性

- 扩展剪贴板历史恢复范围：会扫描可能的 Application Support 位置，以及与 ClipTrace 相关的容器目录，查找旧 SwiftData 历史库。
- 恢复仍然只会导入包含剪迹 `ZCLIPBOARDITEM` 表的数据库，并按剪贴板记录 UUID 去重。
- 这覆盖了升级 macOS 26 前的历史库被留在非标准路径或旧应用容器路径里的情况。
