## en

### Reliability

- Fixed legacy history recovery when the new stable store already contains clips copied after upgrading to 0.9.8.
- ClipTrace now merges missing rows from older SwiftData stores by clipboard-item UUID instead of only copying the old store when the new store is empty.
- Newly copied clips are preserved while older history is imported.

## zh

### 可靠性

- 修复升级到 0.9.8 后，如果新稳定库里已经有新复制记录，旧历史无法恢复的问题。
- 剪迹现在会按剪贴板记录 UUID 从旧 SwiftData 库合并缺失记录，而不是只在新库为空时复制旧库。
- 升级后新复制的记录会保留，旧历史也会被导入。
