## en

### New

- Dwell preview for the menu bar panel and Quick Paste: rest the pointer on a menu bar row — or the keyboard focus / pointer on a Quick Paste row — for a moment and a full preview (text, rich text, image, file, video) pops up beside the panel. Once open it follows your row changes instantly, QuickLook-style, and it never steals keyboard focus from the panel. Each surface has its own toggle and trigger delay (0.5–5 s, default 1 s) in Settings → General → Preview, and protected (masked) clips never auto-preview.
- The Dynamic Island and the menu bar entry are now independent: each has its own switch and turning one off no longer affects the other.

### Fixes

- The app icon now renders correctly on every system: macOS 26 gets a Tahoe-grid compliant icon (no more shrunken art on a grey system plate), while macOS 15 and earlier show the classic die-cut icon again instead of a stray white background plate.
- If the history database cannot be opened at launch (e.g. after a downgrade or disk corruption), the app now quarantines it into a timestamped backup folder and starts with a fresh store instead of crashing on every launch — a one-time notice explains where the old data lives.
- Quick Paste reliably receives keyboard focus when summoned on an external display.
- The Dynamic Island menu fills its black container edge-to-edge, and the macOS 26 menu bar panel no longer shows transparent bands above and below the content.

## zh

### 新功能

- 菜单栏面板与快速粘贴面板新增停留预览：鼠标在菜单栏条目上悬停片刻，或键盘焦点 / 鼠标在快速粘贴条目上停留片刻，即可在面板一侧弹出完整预览（文本、富文本、图片、文件、视频）。预览弹出后切换条目会立即跟随（类似 QuickLook），且绝不抢占面板的键盘焦点。两处均可在「设置 → 通用 → 预览」中独立开关并调整触发时长（0.5–5 秒，默认 1 秒）；受内容保护（已脱敏）的条目不会自动弹出预览。
- 灵动岛与菜单栏入口彻底解耦：各自独立开关，互不影响。

### 修复

- 应用图标在各系统上均正确显示：macOS 26 使用符合 Tahoe 规范的图标（不再被缩小垫上系统灰底板），macOS 15 及更早系统恢复经典异形图标，不再多出一块白色背景板。
- 启动时若历史数据库无法打开（如版本回退或磁盘损坏），会将其隔离到带时间戳的备份目录并以全新数据库启动，不再每次启动即崩溃；应用内会一次性提示旧数据所在位置。
- 修复快速粘贴面板在外接显示器上唤起后拿不到键盘焦点的问题。
- 灵动岛菜单填满黑色容器边缘；macOS 26 菜单栏面板上下不再出现透明条带。
