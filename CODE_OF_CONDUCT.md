# Code of Conduct

ClipTrace is a small open-source project. The contribution standard is simple:

- Be respectful and specific.
- Criticize code, behavior, and tradeoffs, not people.
- Assume sensitive clipboard data may be involved; do not ask people to paste secrets into public issues.
- Keep discussions useful for maintainers and future readers.
- Do not harass, threaten, or deliberately derail contributors.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block participants when behavior makes the project harder or unsafe to maintain.

If you need to report a private security or privacy concern, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
